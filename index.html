Studio Manifesto

WE BUILD SYSTEMS THAT CREATE STORIES.

Rather than building worlds entirely by hand, Bonefauna explores what happens when creatures, genetics, environments, and systems interact.

Our games are designed around simulation and discovery: populations change, traits are inherited, ecosystems develop, and unexpected outcomes become part of the experience.

The goal isn't to simulate nature perfectly. It's to make systems deep enough that players can discover things we didn't explicitly script.

Engineering Methodology

AN AI-NATIVE STUDIO

Bonefauna is being built around a development model where AI is an active engineering partner rather than a separate productivity tool.

ARCHITECTURE

Exploring and maintaining complex game systems and technical designs.

IMPLEMENTATION

Building and iterating on gameplay systems, tools, interfaces, and infrastructure.

RESEARCH

Working through scientific, historical, biological, and technical material.

DOCUMENTATION

Maintaining structured project knowledge, specifications, roadmaps, and development records.

ITERATION

Analyzing existing code, identifying problems, proposing solutions, and implementing changes across a growing codebase.

The human role remains essential: defining the vision, deciding what belongs in the games, evaluating results, and directing the systems we build.

Studio Vision

ONE STUDIO. MANY SYSTEMS.

Bonefauna began with a simple question:

"What happens when games treat living systems as the foundation rather than the decoration?"

That question leads in different directions—from breeding and genetics to ecosystems, microorganisms, extinction, historical systems, and discovery.

Each project is its own game.

Together, they form a broader exploration of simulated life.
